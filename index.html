25. Adicionando Detalhes com Pronomes Relativos

25.1 Quem e O Quê: Pronomes Relativos (Who, Which, That) 👤❓

Pronomes Relativos introduzem Orações Relativas (Relative Clauses), que são frases que dão mais informações sobre um substantivo mencionado anteriormente. Eles conectam duas ideias em uma única frase mais complexa.

  • WHO (/huː/): Refere-se a pessoas.
    Ex: The woman who lives next door is a doctor.
    (A mulher que mora ao lado é médica.)
  • WHICH (/wɪtʃ/): Refere-se a coisas ou animais.
    Ex: This is the book which I recommended.
    (Este é o livro que eu recomendei.)
  • THAT (/ðæt/): Pode se referir a pessoas, coisas ou animais. É frequentemente usado de forma mais informal e pode substituir "who" ou "which" em muitos casos (em orações essenciais).
    Ex (Pessoa): The man that called you is my boss.
    (O homem que te ligou é meu chefe.)

25.2 De Quem é a Posse: "Whose" 🔑

WHOSE (/huːz/) é um pronome relativo possessivo. Ele mostra que algo pertence à pessoa (ou, às vezes, coisa) mencionada anteriormente.

  • Ex: That's the artist whose paintings are famous.
    (Aquele é o artista cujas pinturas são famosas.)
  • Ex: I have a friend whose brother is an actor.
    (Eu tenho um amigo cujo irmão é ator.)

25.3 Onde e Quando: "Where" e "When" como Pronomes Relativos 📍⏰

"Where" (/wer/) e "When" (/wen/) também podem funcionar como pronomes relativos para introduzir orações que dão mais informações sobre lugares e tempos.

  • WHERE: Refere-se a lugares.
    Ex: This is the city where I was born.
    (Esta é a cidade onde eu nasci.)
  • WHEN: Refere-se a tempos.
    Ex: I remember the day when we first met.
    (Eu me lembro do dia em que nos conhecemos pela primeira vez.)

25.4 Orações Relativas Essenciais e Não Essenciais

As orações relativas podem ser de dois tipos:

  • Defining Relative Clauses (Essenciais): Fornecem informação essencial para identificar sobre quem ou o que estamos falando. Não usamos vírgulas e podemos usar "that".
    Ex: The man who lives next door is a famous actor.
    (A informação "who lives next door" é essencial para saber de qual homem estamos falando).
  • Non-defining Relative Clauses (Não Essenciais): Fornecem informação adicional, mas não essencial. Se removermos essa oração, a frase principal ainda faz sentido. Usamos vírgulas para separá-las e não podemos usar "that".
    Ex: My brother, who is an engineer, lives in London.
    (Já sabemos de quem estamos falando - "meu irmão". A informação de que ele é engenheiro é extra).

Tarefa Extra ✍️

Translate the Text

Read the short story below. Pay close attention to how the relative pronouns (who, which, whose, where, when) are used to connect ideas and add details. Then, try to translate it into Portuguese.

My grandfather is a man who loves to tell stories. He often talks about the small town where he grew up. It had an old main street, which was always busy on Saturdays. He remembers a baker, whose bread was the best in the entire region. "He was a man that always had a smile for everyone," my grandfather says. The stories that he told were legendary.

I'll never forget the day when he first showed me the old photo album. The album, which he keeps in a wooden box, is full of black and white pictures. He pointed to a young woman. "This is your grandmother, whom I met at a summer festival." He described the festival, where musicians played all night long.

He showed me a picture of his first car, which was a beautiful old convertible. He spoke of the long drives he took on roads that are now major highways. He has a friend, whose letters he has kept for over sixty years. This friend is someone who now lives in another country.

He says the moment when he felt the proudest was when my father was born. The hospital where my father was born is now a museum. It's a place that holds many memories for our family. Every story he tells is a piece of a puzzle, the puzzle which makes up his incredible life.

Quiz

What is special about the baker from the grandfather's town?

  1. He was a man who owned the entire town.
  2. He was the person whom my grandfather disliked.
  3. He was the baker whose bread was considered the best.
  4. He was the man which sold old cars.
  5. He lived in the house where my grandfather was born.
Ver Resposta do Quiz
Resposta Correta: 3. He was the baker whose bread was considered the best.
